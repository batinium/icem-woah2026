# AI-Assistance Disclosure

Use this disclosure in `draft.tex` and in the submission system's Responsible
NLP / AI-assistance questionnaire. The wording is non-identifying and does not
name any human author.

Suggested submission text:

> OpenAI Codex/ChatGPT assisted with code implementation and debugging,
> language polishing and text-flow edits of author-controlled material, local
> verification runs, and bibliography/metadata checks. The authors made the
> research decisions, designed and interpreted the experiments, reviewed the
> AI-assisted material, verified the experiments, citations, and claims, and
> take full responsibility for the final paper. No AI system is listed as an
> author.

For camera-ready, keep or move the same disclosure according to the final venue
instructions.
